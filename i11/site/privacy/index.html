This page explains, in simple words, what {{APP_NAME}} does with your information. Short version: your data stays on your phone.

What we store

Your focus sessions, nights, alarms and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up.

No backup

Your app data is not backed up to the cloud, and it is not copied to a new phone. If you change phones, you start fresh.

Android only: which app is open

On Android, to block apps and count night phone use, the app reads which app is open. It never reads what is inside that app.

Ads

The morning report shows one ad a day from Google’s ad service (AdMob). With your consent, the ad service may use an advertising ID. This is a resettable ID that your phone gives to ad services. You can change your consent any time in Settings → Privacy and data.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, it can send an anonymous crash report so we can fix bugs. It includes the app version, phone model, OS version and what went wrong. It contains no names, emails, app lists, session data, advertising IDs or IP addresses. You can turn crash reports off in Settings.

Feedback emails

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send.

Permissions we never use

The app never uses your microphone, camera, contacts, location, files or phone state.

Delete all data

Go to Settings → Privacy and data → “Delete all data”. This removes everything the app has stored.

Age

The app is for adults 18 and over.
