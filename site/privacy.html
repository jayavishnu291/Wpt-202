At WPT 202, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. About WPT 202

WPT 202 is a focus, night routine, and mission alarm app designed to help users build better phone habits.

2. Information and Data

WPT 202 is designed to work without requiring users to create an account. Personal app information is stored on the user's device.

3. Data Storage

The app is designed to keep personal app data on the user's device rather than storing it on a company server.

4. Advertising

WPT 202 may display an advertisement through Google AdMob in the morning report, according to the app's advertising rules. Advertising providers may process certain information as described in their own privacy policies.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Usage analytics are not included in Release 1. Anonymous crash reporting is used with an opt-out option.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect user information through appropriate design and security practices.

7. Third-Party Services

Certain services, such as advertising and crash reporting, may be provided by third parties. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its features change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
